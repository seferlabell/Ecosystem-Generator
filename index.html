LOON LAKE FIELD TEAM · LESSON 3 EXTENSION

Ecosystem Event Generator

A closed, teacher-driven tool. Spin up a Minnesota ecosystem and a climate or disturbance event, then have the Field Team use their food web as evidence to argue who survives. Nothing is typed to an outside service and no student data is collected.

Choose an ecosystem and event, or press “Surprise me,” then Generate.
How this uses AI. Two ways to run it. This built version is a closed generator: it recombines curated, teacher-checked content, so it is safe for young students and works offline. A second option is a live-AI teacher co-pilot, where the teacher prompts a general AI model to invent new scenarios and arguments. The live version is more flexible but is teacher-run only and must be checked for accuracy, because general AI tools are 13-plus and can make mistakes. Both are documented in the assignment write-up.
